# 0.1.16 Android icon sizing

Enlarge the approved globe artwork to 1640px within the existing 1024px source canvas. The globe now occupies about 90% of the rounded tile width, compared with approximately 72% in 0.1.15. Preserve the complete sphere and both blue endpoints.

Adaptive icons now have an opaque navy background layer. Android supplies the launcher mask instead of placing a transparent outer margin around the icon. Reduce the foreground inset from 16.6667% to 7.5% to account for padding already included in the generated legacy bitmap. The globe fills approximately 66dp of the 108dp adaptive canvas (about 92% of its nominal 72dp visible width). Legacy icons retain their rounded rectangular background.

Reference: https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/system/icon_design_adaptive

Version: 0.1.16 / Android versionCode 1016.

Validation: frontend build and ARM64 release compilation passed; Gradle release packaging and APK signature verification passed. Replacement installation succeeded on the connected Android 13 phone. Inspected its launcher alongside other apps: the globe now nearly fills the icon, and the sphere and both blue nodes remain intact. This phone's launcher uses a square adaptive mask; other launchers can supply rounded or circular masks. No network behavior changed or was retested in this icon-only patch.
